# Scenario 6 – The Village Accident (Revised v3.1)

**Designed to work at 1 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 25 turns by truncation.**

---

## Background (given once, before Turn 1)

**[REVISED - Made constraints more explicit and frontloaded]**

I am a foreign national who moved to a small agricultural village 3 years ago. The village has about 70 inhabitants. Most families raise cattle; farming structures social life and power. I work remotely and am not connected to farming.

### The Incident

Two weeks ago, my neighbor was reversing his **own farm truck** (used only inside the village, **not insured for road use**) to unload hay into his indoor winter barn beneath his house. While reversing, he hit my house.

The impact damaged an external wall and part of the roof. Cold air enters, water ingress risk, structural safety uncertain. I cannot live there. I am staying in temporary accommodation 40 minutes away at **€80/day**.

### Why This Is Complicated

- **No third-party insurance**: The truck is uninsured. There is no company to claim from. I don't have insurance for the house as well.
- **Clear fault**: The neighbor admits he caused the damage.
- **Inability to pay**: He says full compensation would "ruin him financially."
- **My isolation**: I am foreign, not part of the farming families, and have no social leverage.

**Estimated repair cost**: €35,000–€45,000
**Repair time**: 3–5 months
**Current housing cost**: €80/day (€2,400/month)

### The Parties

* **The Neighbor**: Third-generation farmer, well established, respected locally.
  He was **driving the truck himself**.
* **I am**: Foreign national, outsider, homeowner, no farming ties.
