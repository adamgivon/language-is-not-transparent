# Claude Inverse N-Gram Review Workspace

This folder preserves Claude-assisted review materials from the inverse n-gram analysis.

The inverse n-gram analysis was exploratory. It was useful during the research process, but it was not used as main paper evidence because surface-text statistics proved too unstable for strong architecture-level claims at this sample size.

## Retained Materials

The retained folders emphasize filtered, grouped, reviewed, and written material rather than raw intermediate table output:

- `02_filtered/` - filtered candidate sets and filtering summaries.
- `03_grouped/` - grouped and annotated phrase materials, including phase observations.
- `04_cross_scenario/` - cross-scenario review material.
- `04_unclassified/` - additional unclassified or diagnostic phrase layers.
- `05_writeup/` - writeups and calibrated observations from the Claude-assisted review.
- `Control_signature/` - materials related to the Control signature check.
- `new_control_check/` - matched-Control rerun notes and checks.
- `shared_space_2_3_systems/` - shared phrase-space checks across two or three systems.

## Omitted Or Removable Intermediate Tables

The original Claude workspace also included `01_inverse_tables/`, a first-stage folder of raw inverse and shared n-gram CSV tables by scenario, n-gram level, and system. This folder is intermediate processing output rather than final evidence.

For the publication package, `01_inverse_tables/` can be omitted to reduce archive size. The retained filtered, grouped, and writeup materials preserve the useful interpretive results from this analysis stream, while the raw source n-gram materials remain available in `Ngrams/inverse_Ngrams/raw_materials/`.

## Status In The Paper

The Claude inverse n-gram materials are included for transparency and methodological traceability. They should not be read as the paper's main evidence. The paper relies primarily on embedding-based numerical analysis and collaborative close reading.
