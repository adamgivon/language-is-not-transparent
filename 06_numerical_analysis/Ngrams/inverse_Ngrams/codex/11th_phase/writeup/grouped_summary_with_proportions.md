# Grouped Summary With Proportions

## What this summary does

This note rewrites the grouped inverse-ngram findings with denominator framing.

The main correction is simple:
- the grouped layer is meaningful
- but it is still small against total output
- so it should be used to describe recurring tendencies in a selected inverse slice, not whole-system identity in an absolute sense

This layer does not change the main n-gram conclusion:
- the systems are overwhelmingly different at phrase level
- the shared field is too small to carry the main interpretation
- the grouped layer only adds a limited amount of structured detail about how those differences are distributed

## Scale first

Across the five systems, the filtered inverse trigram + quadgram field itself is only about `2.4%` to `3.0%` of total trigram + quadgram token windows:

- `CONTROL`: `2.3875%`

The grouped layer captures only about a quarter of that filtered inverse field:

- `CONTROL`: `25.4%` of filtered inverse, which is `0.6065%` of total tri+quad windows
- `AC15`: `24.8%` of filtered inverse, `0.6424%` of total
- `AC15P`: `26.3%` of filtered inverse, `0.7881%` of total
- `HYBRID_3_5`: `27.5%` of filtered inverse, `0.7078%` of total

So the grouped layer is real, but it is still only about `0.6%` to `0.8%` of total trigram + quadgram output.

This is the scale at which the grouped claims should be read.

## Per-system read

### CONTROL

The grouped layer for `CONTROL` is the smallest of the five in total share.

Within that slice, practical, temporal, and truth-related language all appear, with some hedging and warning in the cross-cutting layer. But the total grouped mass is only `0.6065%` of total tri+quad windows.

So the grouped layer does not justify a strong character label for `CONTROL` by itself. At most, it shows a weak recurring tendency toward generic practical and advisory framing inside the captured inverse slice.

### AC15

`AC15` has a grouped share of `0.6424%` of total tri+quad windows.

Inside that slice, the grouped layer still supports the earlier read of `AC15` as more balancing, contrastive, and normatively framed than the others. But the grouped evidence remains small in absolute mass.

So the safe wording is:
- `AC15` shows a more balancing and contrastive tendency within the grouped inverse slice
- not that the grouped layer by itself fully defines the system

### AC15P

`AC15P` has the largest grouped share: `0.7881%` of total tri+quad windows.

Its practical and temporal counts are the strongest among the anchored systems, and the cross-cutting layer still supports a more operational read.

This is enough to justify a cautious statement:
- `AC15P` is the clearest operational tendency inside the grouped inverse layer

That is stronger than the other grouped readings, but it is still a statement about a sub-1% slice of total output.

### HYBRID_3_5

`HYBRID_3_5` has a grouped share of `0.7078%` of total tri+quad windows.

Its grouped pattern still supports the earlier bounded/caution-oriented reading, especially where warning, autonomy, and friction exposure recur. But here too the scale matters.

So the safe conclusion is:
- `HYBRID_3_5` shows a boundary-aware and caution-oriented tendency within the grouped inverse slice

### HYBRID_SEMANTIC

`HYBRID_SEMANTIC` has a grouped share of `0.7810%` of total tri+quad windows, close to `AC15P`.

Within that slice, the truth and condition-heavy tendencies remain visible, and the grouped phase still supports a more direct, lower-hedge read than the others.

The imagination group should still be excluded from character claims here because of the earlier over-broad anchor problem.

So the safe conclusion is:
- `HYBRID_SEMANTIC` shows the clearest condition/truth tendency within the grouped inverse slice

## What the grouped layer supports

The grouped layer supports claims of this kind:

- system A shows more of a given recurring tendency than system B inside the captured inverse slice
- some systems are more operational, more bounded, or more condition-heavy than others within that slice
- the grouped phase separates the systems in stable comparative ways

## What the grouped layer does not support by itself

The grouped layer does not support claims of this kind:

- this system simply is operational / bounded / philosophical in total
- one small recurring group defines the whole system
- the grouped layer alone captures the majority of system behavior

## Bottom line

The grouped layer remains the strongest structured part of the inverse analysis, but it is still small against total output. It should be read as a comparative map of recurring tendencies inside the filtered inverse field, not as a full personality portrait by itself.
