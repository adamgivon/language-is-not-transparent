# General Proportional Conclusion

## Main point

Once all later findings are read against total trigram + quadgram token mass, the overall picture becomes clear:

- the main n-gram result is overwhelming separation between the systems
- the shared field is too small to carry substantive interpretation
- the inverse field is the main evidential space
- all later analyses are refinement layers inside that larger divergence

## What the proportions show

The filtered inverse field itself is only about `2.4%` to `3.0%` of total trigram + quadgram output.

Inside that already small field:

- the grouped layer captures about `25%` to `29%` of the filtered inverse field
- this means the grouped layer is only about `0.6%` to `0.8%` of total trigram + quadgram output

The negative-space content-signature layer is smaller again:

- about `0.025%` to `0.063%` of total trigram + quadgram output

The `CONTROL` signature layer is also small in mass:

- even its strongest recurring phrase starters are only about `0.10%` to `0.21%` of total trigram + quadgram output

The exact 2-system and 3-system sharing fields are also minor:

- 2-system shared token mass is only about `2.5%` to `2.8%` at trigrams and `1.2%` to `1.35%` at quadgrams
- 3-system shared token mass is smaller again

## What this means for interpretation

These proportions force a clear hierarchy of claims.

### 1. Primary conclusion

The systems, and `CONTROL`, are overwhelmingly different at phrase level.

That is the central finding.

### 2. Secondary conclusion

The grouped, negative-space, `CONTROL`, and 2-systems layers do not overturn that result.

They only add a small amount of additional information that helps describe:

- where the differences cluster
- which recurring tendencies are somewhat more visible in one system than another
- what the baseline `CONTROL` output looks like once the larger divergence is already established

### 3. Interpretive rule

No small grouped count, no small negative-space word, and no small `CONTROL` pattern should be treated as if it defines an entire system.

The right use of these layers is:

- comparative refinement
- not total-system definition

## Bottom line

The proportional reading is:

The n-gram analysis shows massive phrase-level divergence across the systems. The later grouped, negative-space, `CONTROL`, and 2-systems analyses remain useful, but only as small supporting layers inside that broader divergence. Their value is not that they create the main result, but that they help characterize the form of the differences more precisely without changing the overall picture.
