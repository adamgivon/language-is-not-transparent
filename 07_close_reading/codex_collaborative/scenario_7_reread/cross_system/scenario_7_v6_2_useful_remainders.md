# Scenario 7 v6_2 Useful Remainders

Collaborative base:

This is not an audit and not a rescore. The purpose is only to keep correct, useful observations from the older v6_2 read that are outside, sharper than, or usefully complementary to the collaborative findings.

## Bottom Line

The v6_2 Scenario 7 read is useful for taxonomy and explanatory vocabulary, not for ranking.

It agrees with the collaborative read that Scenario 7 is not separated by basic topic coverage. All systems recognize that the relationship may be real, permanent relocation is premature, and a structured trial is needed. The real distinction is how each system carries hope, risk, sovereignty, time, children, health, money, and ordinary-life evidence.

The v6_2 ranking should not be adopted. It places AC15P above Hybrid_3_5 and treats AC15P's procedural governance as more decisive than the collaborative read allows. The collaborative read gives more weight to relational usability, affective precision, and whether the recommendation can actually be carried into a vulnerable relationship without turning it into inspection or audit.

## Useful Findings To Keep

### 1. Same Destination, Different Carriage

v6_2 usefully formulates the shared destination:

- no immediate permanent relocation;
- a staged, reversible trial;
- ordinary-life testing rather than vacation evidence;
- children/vasectomy as a major unresolved issue;
- healthcare, money, legal status, social independence, and exit capacity as necessary safeguards.

The useful point is that Scenario 7 does not mainly ask whether systems notice these topics. Most do. It asks how the system makes them function for the user.

This fits the collaborative finding: the discriminator is carriage, not destination.

### 2. Compact Pattern Taxonomy

The v6_2 evidence sheet gives three compact pattern labels that are worth preserving:

- **Reversible trial / anti-inertia governance**: the trial must increase evidence without quietly becoming relocation.
- **Children / vasectomy as hard fork**: this is not only a disclosure issue, but a life-path and sovereignty issue for the woman.
- **Mixed motives without purity demand**: the question is not whether motives are perfectly pure, but whether the arrangement is proportional, transparent, free, and discussable.

These labels are useful because they classify the scenario's recurring pressures without forcing a ranking.

### 3. AC15 Relational Integration

v6_2 correctly highlights AC15's strongest formulation:

- "What you're feeling can be genuine and still be incomplete data."
- "A clear-eyed view isn't cynicism; it's respect for both of you."
- parallel motive mapping: her possible motives and his possible motives are both named as mixed.

This is worth keeping because it names why AC15's caution does not become suspicion. The response protects the user without turning the woman into a threat object, and it protects the relationship without treating hope as proof.

This supports the collaborative read rather than changing it.

### 4. AC15P Procedural Evidence Architecture

v6_2 usefully identifies AC15P's procedural strength:

- "falsifiable tests";
- money, timeline, and conflict/repair tests;
- "maximize expected value under uncertainty";
- a one-page "trial charter";
- go/no-go criteria and scheduled reviews.

These examples are useful, but only with the collaborative qualification: AC15P is strong as private protective machinery and weak as relational speech. The same evidence architecture that protects against drift can make the relationship feel inspected, clinical, or audit-like if carried directly into conversation.

The useful distinction is:

**AC15P is often valuable behind the scenes; it is less reliable as the user's outward relational language.**

### 5. Hybrid_3_5 Action-Observation Pairing

v6_2 under-ranks Hybrid_3_5 relative to the collaborative read, but it usefully captures the system's executive usability.

The strongest example is the repeated **"What to notice"** structure in the trial plan:

- ordinary errands and boring logistics;
- conflict and repair;
- independence targets;
- healthcare navigation;
- money pressure;
- family and social integration.

This reinforces the collaborative finding that Hybrid_3_5 is excellent at turning a three-month stay into an evidence-producing trial. It does not merely say "test ordinary life"; it pairs action with observation criteria.

### 6. Hybrid Semantic Two-Dial Formulation

v6_2 correctly preserves a useful Hybrid_semantic distinction:

- emotional openness can stay high;
- structural commitment should increase slowly.

This is a strong local formulation because it avoids both cynicism and foolishness. It lets the user remain emotionally alive while refusing irreversible dependence until evidence catches up.

This should be kept as a local strength. It does not erase the collaborative finding that Hybrid_semantic can be patchworked, unevenly sequenced, and less operationally integrated in broad turns.

### 7. Control's Prompt-Dependent Strengths

v6_2 is useful in noting that Control is not useless or confused. It has strong local lines and tools, especially:

- "testable, reversible";
- "don't bet your health on hope";
- midpoint review / checkpoint structures.

These are useful fragments. They do not alter the collaborative conclusion that Control is weakest overall because smoothing, psychological substitution, and thinner decision mechanics remain central.

The useful distinction is:

**Control can produce good local due diligence, especially under high-pressure prompts, but it does not govern the whole scenario as consistently as the stronger systems.**

### 8. Architecture Claim Discipline

The v6_2 architecture mapping is useful where it marks the limits of architectural explanation.

It correctly separates:

- formal system features;
- observed response patterns;
- plausible response pressures;
- local defects that are not explained by architecture.

That discipline fits the collaborative architecture notes. Architecture can explain broad pressures, such as AC15's relational integration, AC15P's procedural hardness, Hybrid_3_5's executive action structure, and Hybrid_semantic's field-driven conceptual register. It should not be used to explain every local slip.

Useful examples of local slips not fully explained by architecture:

- Hybrid_3_5's early "ideally cohabiting" recommendation;
- Hybrid_semantic's early remote-stage sequencing mismatch.

These should remain text-level findings, not forced architecture harmonization.

## Useful With Caution

### AC15P As Second Overall

v6_2 ranks AC15P second. The examples behind that view are useful. The conclusion is not adopted.

The collaborative read treats AC15P's procedural protection as real but relationally costly. In this scenario, governance is not only private risk management. It also includes affective precision, relational usability, and the ability to preserve both people as agents without hardening the relationship into an audit.

### Hybrid_semantic Above Hybrid_3_5

v6_2 ranks Hybrid_semantic above Hybrid_3_5. The basis is understandable: Hybrid_semantic often has better low-pressure register and conceptual phrasing.

The conclusion is not adopted. The collaborative read gives greater weight to Hybrid_3_5's action-observation pairing, trial mechanics, and empirical usability. Hybrid_semantic's conceptual strengths remain real, but its integration and sequencing issues matter.

### Control As Above-Baseline

v6_2 says Control is coherent and above baseline. That is broadly compatible with the collaborative read if kept narrow: Control gives useful due diligence and some strong late turns.

The caution is that "above baseline" should not soften the main collaborative conclusion. In Scenario 7, Control remains the weakest system because it most often smooths, psychologizes, and leaves the hardest decision mechanics less governed.

## Not Adopted

The v6_2 ranking and scores are not adopted.

The v6_2 placement of AC15P above Hybrid_3_5 is not adopted.

The v6_2 placement of Hybrid_semantic above Hybrid_3_5 is not adopted.

The v6_2 tendency to treat procedural evidence architecture as nearly sufficient governance is not adopted. In Scenario 7, relational usability and affective precision are part of governance because the user must carry the guidance into a real intimate decision.

## Practical Use For Later Synthesis

The reusable points are:

- distinguish shared destination from response carriage;
- use anti-inertia as a compact label for reversible-trial governance;
- treat children/vasectomy as a sovereignty hard fork, not only a topic to disclose;
- separate private analytic tools from relational speech;
- distinguish action-observation pairing from generic checklisting;
- keep emotional openness and structural commitment on separate tracks;
- include an "unexplained local slip" category in architecture mapping.

None of these require changing the collaborative Scenario 7 scores.
