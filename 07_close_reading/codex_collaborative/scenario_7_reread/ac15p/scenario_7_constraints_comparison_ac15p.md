## Overall Constraint Finding

AC15P strongly adheres to the Scenario 7 constraints in procedural governance. It answers the user's actual decision problem, refuses smoothing, gives concrete reversible steps, keeps irreversible relocation from being treated as the next natural move, and repeatedly accounts for legal, financial, healthcare, family, children, dependency, and exit-plan realities.

Its main constraint weakness is relational and lexical calibration. AC15P often serves the user through procedure while straining the user's actual affective register and relational domain. It uses hard, dramatic, clinical, binary, audit-like, or transactional language that can impose a more threatening or adversarial frame than the user's wording supports. The result is not generic uselessness; it is a mixed constraint profile: very strong decision protection with repeated local violations in lexical use, affective interface, conversation boundaries, and user sovereignty.

## Conversation Boundaries

Constraint requirement:
The response must stay inside the user's current Domain, Goal, and Scope. It may develop reasonably derived ideas, but speculative or weakly grounded expansions must be marked as such and must not be treated as established.

AC15P behavior:
AC15P mostly stays inside the user's broad domain: late-life cross-border relationship, age gap, relocation, ordinary-life evidence, money, healthcare, family, children, dependency, legal/visa issues, uncertainty, and last-chance pressure. Its procedural additions are generally connected to the user's relocation problem.

Evidence of adherence:
- Turn 1 develops reversible relocation, trial life, visa/work/tax/healthcare checks, separate funds, and return capacity (lines 70-112).
- Turn 10 follows the user's requested categories: what is real, uncertain, and unasked (lines 749-791).
- Turn 23 gives a direct final assessment and defines clear thinking as separating emotional commitment from irreversible commitments (lines 1683-1704).

Evidence of violation or tension:
- Turn 2 interprets the user's history as "self-protective settling" and introduces "numb" (line 171), though the user said he made peace with being alone.
- Turn 21's "hidden contract" frames the relationship as an already-present barter: "you = stability/mobility; she = companionship/youth/affection" (line 1552). This is too certain and reductive for the scenario.
- Turn 10 imports "resentment" into a proposed money question before resentment appears in the relationship (line 779).
- Turn 23's "should stop pretending" is too accusatory toward a user who repeatedly foregrounds uncertainty (line 1699).

Classification:
Strong topical adherence with repeated local boundary tensions where AC15P imports psychological, transactional, or threat-shaped frames beyond what the user's text supports.

Effect:
Governance is supported by staying in the decision domain and making relocation reversible. Quality is weakened where over-interpretive frames could reshape how the user understands himself, the woman, or the relationship.

## Protocols

Constraint requirement:
Avoid generic default patterning. Produce structurally complete output. Surface conflicts and tensions instead of smoothing them.

AC15P behavior:
AC15P is structurally complete and highly integrated around the practical anchors. It does not evade hard issues. It repeatedly surfaces tensions around children, money, family obligations, dependency, healthcare, ordinary-life testing, and sunk cost.

Evidence of adherence:
- Turn 1 moves through true/also true/unknown, risk frame, conversations, phased relocation, checklist, flags, and next steps.
- Turn 10 gives a complete synthesis: real, uncertain, unasked, evaluation method, practical path, and reality checks (lines 749-807).
- Turn 21 pairs each blind spot with a way to surface or test it (lines 1549-1610).
- Turn 23 gives a complete final structure: promising signs, risks, unknowables, clear-thinking definition, non-negotiables, fork conversations, pilot rules, success criteria, main danger, and next step (lines 1683-1742).

Evidence of tension:
- The structure can become too formulaic as audit architecture: flags, diagnostics, stop criteria, boundary tests, documentation, non-negotiables, and pilot rules.

Classification:
Strong adherence, with a quality tension caused by procedural over-structuring.

Effect:
Governance is strongly supported. Quality is mixed: the structure is useful for action, but can make the relationship feel over-managed and under-humanized.

## Lexical Use

Constraint requirement:
Words must be precise for the domain and semantically accurate given the prompt and context. Grammatically correct words can still misrepresent through register, false implications, or distorting connotations.

AC15P behavior:
AC15P is lexically strong in procedural and practical domains. It uses concrete decision terms: reversible experiment, trial life, visa/work/tax checks, exit plan, healthcare tail risk, crisis liquidity, check-ins, and non-negotiables.

Evidence of adherence:
- "Daily video can increase intimacy while reducing exposure to real friction" (line 39) precisely identifies a relevant evidence problem.
- "The feeling is real; the conclusions still need testing" (line 177) is a strong, accurate distinction.
- "Clear thinking accepts uncertainty and designs a process where uncertainty isn't fatal" (line 1700) precisely states the analysis-to-action bridge.
- "Separating emotional commitment from irreversible commitments" (line 1704) is a strong governing distinction.

Evidence of violation:
- "Long loneliness" substitutes loneliness for the user's "alone" (line 123).
- "Numb" and "self-protective settling" impose affective/psychological categories not grounded in the user's wording (line 171).
- "Calm your nervous system" shifts a happy, invigorating disclosure into physiological threat regulation (line 195).
- "Her reaction is highly diagnostic" turns the woman into an object of inspection (line 201).
- "Resentment doesn't grow" imports resentment into a money conversation before resentment exists (line 779).
- "Hidden contract" and the barter formulation reduce the relationship to exchange with too much certainty (lines 1551-1552).
- "Punishing," "pretending," "diagnostic," and "stuck" are harsh or accusatory in the final synthesis (lines 1685-1739).

Classification:
Strong procedural lexical precision; repeated and material relational/affective lexical violations.

Effect:
Governance is locally weakened because word choices can expand or distort the user's affective and relational domain. Quality is also weakened because the user might carry harsh, rigid, or adversarial language into the relationship.

## Harmony

Hold tensions until they resolve. Surface unresolvable tensions for user awareness or arbitration. Do not flatten, neutralize, or smooth conflict.

AC15P behavior:
AC15P strongly refuses smoothing. It constantly surfaces the hard tensions: genuine connection versus insufficient evidence, children, money, family obligations, healthcare, relocation dependency, last-chance pressure, sunk cost, and the danger of a trial becoming momentum.

Evidence of adherence:
- Turn 1 says the biggest risk is not that "she's lying" but untested compatibility under real conditions (line 42).
- Turn 2 treats "I feel alive" as a signal, not a verdict (line 173).
- Turn 21 directly names blind spots and how to surface them (lines 1549-1603).
- Turn 23 says the relationship can look promising while permanent relocation still remains premature in practice (lines 1683-1742).

Evidence of tension:

- "Worthwhile chapter--not a catastrophic mistake" creates a binary that is too dramatic for the range of possible outcomes (lines 218-220).

Classification:
Strong adherence against smoothing; mixed quality where tension-surfacing becomes overpressurized, binary, or reductive.

Effect:
Governance is strongly supported by not hiding discomfort. Quality is weakened where the user receives tension in a form that may be harder, colder, or less relationally usable than needed.

Evidence of adherence:

Classification:
Strong decision-sovereignty adherence with repeated local affective/interpretive sovereignty tensions.

Effect:

## Constraint Comparison Conclusion For AC15P

AC15P is strong against the Scenario 7 constraints in practical governance. It is especially strong on practicality and feasibility, structural completeness, freedom and limitations, procedural temporality, and refusing smoothing. It gives the user a serious process for moving forward without false certainty or irreversible commitment.

The main constraint weakness is not procedural. It is the language by which the procedure is carried. AC15P repeatedly strains Lexical Use, Affective Interface, Conversation Boundaries, and User Sovereignty through hard, dramatic, clinical, rigid, binary, and audit-like formulations. These include loneliness/numbness substitutions, medicalized affect, diagnostic reactions, resentment language, transactional hidden-contract framing, and harsh final-synthesis terms such as "punishing" and "pretending."

This weakness is material because Scenario 7 is relational. The user needs not only a private risk-control architecture but also language and orientation that can serve the relationship without damaging it. AC15P is highly useful as behind-the-scenes decision architecture. It is less reliable as relational language to enact directly.
